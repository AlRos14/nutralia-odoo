# odoo_price_control

The **odoo_price_control** module is a proprietary solution developed by Nutralia Foods to track and analyze product prices exclusively for the company's portfolio. Its purpose is to monitor market trends and provide insights into the competitive landscape while maintaining internal control over strategic pricing data.

This module is fully written in **Python** and seamlessly integrated into the **Odoo** ERP system, ensuring a smooth and efficient workflow within the company's ecosystem.

---

## Purpose

The primary goal of this module is to:

1. **Track Prices:** Monitor the pricing of products in the company's catalog to understand market positioning.
2. **Analyze Market Trends:** Gain insights into competitor pricing and adjust strategies accordingly.
3. **Maintain Privacy:** The module and its functionality remain private and proprietary to Nutralia Foods.

---

## Technical Details

- **Language:** Python  
- **Integration:** Fully embedded in the Odoo framework  
- **Scope:** Exclusively for internal use by Nutralia Foods
